FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does payment work?

After approving the quote, you pay 100% upfront via Wise or PayPal. Product price + commission + shipping included.

How do I know it's authentic?

All products are bought in official boutiques. Original invoice, receipt and photos are sent on every purchase.

What are the delivery times?

Tracked Colissimo International to Turkey, usually 5–10 business days.

Are there customs duties?

Imports are subject to Turkish customs rules (~20%). We manage the process transparently and inform you clearly upfront — duties are the client's responsibility.

Are returns possible?

The boutiques' return policies apply. For a wrong size or colour we do our utmost to find the best solution with you.
