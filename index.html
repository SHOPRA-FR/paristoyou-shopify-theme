FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does payment work?

After we validate your list together, you pay a deposit covering the estimated purchases + my provisional fee (Wise, PayPal or bank transfer).

How do I know it's authentic?

All products are bought in the official boutiques of the Paris outlets. Original invoice, receipt and photos are sent with every purchase.

Where do you deliver?

Across Europe and worldwide, with a tracked parcel to the address on your account.

Are there customs duties?

Depending on your country, import duties may apply on arrival — they remain the client's responsibility. I manage the process transparently and inform you upfront.

Are returns possible?

The boutiques' return policies apply. For a wrong size or colour we do our utmost to find the best solution with you.
